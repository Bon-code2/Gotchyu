About Gotchyu

Gotchyu is a small tool for people who want to understand how their writing reads. Paste a draft, get a score, and see the exact sentences behind it.

It looks at rhythm, repeated phrasing, stock wording and how uniform the sentences are. Those are patterns, not fingerprints, so the result is a prompt to take a second look at your draft.

You can use it to polish your own work before you send it, or to spot the passages that sound generic.

  • What a score meansHow strongly the text shows patterns common in AI writing.
  • What it doesn’t meanIt can’t prove who wrote something. Please don’t use a score alone to accuse anyone.
  • Your textDescribe here what you store and for how long. Link it to your privacy policy.
  • Best resultsUse 300 words or more. Short passages give unreliable scores.
